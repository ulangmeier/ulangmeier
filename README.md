## 📈 Strategic Overview & Mission

I lead the architectural evolution at **[Langmeier Software](https://www.langmeier-software.com)**, focusing on the **long-term value and technical resilience** of our software assets. My mission is to bridge proven enterprise stability with high-performance, future-proof technologies.

- **Strategic Migration:** Executing the transition of core backup engines from legacy environments to **Rust**, ensuring memory safety and 10-year+ maintainability.
- **Performance Engineering:** Developing proprietary **Adaptive Multiscaling** algorithms to maximize hardware throughput (PTR) and maintain market leadership.
- **Architectural Efficiency:** Pivoting our **aBusiness Suite (Cloud ERP/CRM)** toward lean, high-performance architectures to reduce technical debt and increase operational scalability.
- **Ethical AI Integration:** Implementing [Responsible Coding 2025](https://github.com/ulangmeier/responsible-coding-2025) standards to leverage AI-assisted development while maintaining strict IP security and code quality.

## 📐 Engineering Principles for Investment Security

- Value-Driven Architecture: Every line of code must **reduce debt** or **increase scalability**.
- Sustainability: Using proven **enterprise standards** to ensure the next engineer can take over seamlessly.
- KISS (Keep It Simple, Stupid): **Minimizing complexity** to reduce maintenance costs and operational risk.
- **Security by Design**: In data protection, integrity and security are non-negotiable assets.
- **Production-readiness** means full visibility into system health from day one.
